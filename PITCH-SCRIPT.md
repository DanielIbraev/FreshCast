## Slide 4: "How one forecast is built" (1:24–1:34)
> "Normal waste, times the day, times your kitchen. Then prep 63 percent of that less, and keep the rest as a buffer."

## Slide 5: "Works for any kind of kitchen" (1:34–1:46)
> "On simulated kitchens with hidden habits, it's 84 to 86 percent accurate. That's 16 to 29 percent fewer misses than an owner with perfect memory."

## Slide 6: "One restaurant, one year" (1:46–1:53)
> "For one restaurant, that's about 2,000 meals' worth of food a year, in simulation."

## Slide 7: "At just 1% of America's independent restaurants" (1:53–2:00)
