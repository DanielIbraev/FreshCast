## Full prompt (paste this first)

PRESERVE: Keep the original camera position, framing, lens, body motion, hand movements, head turns, lip movement, speech and timing exactly as filmed, frame for frame. Keep the man's own face, hair, skin tone and build from the source video in every frame. Do not change his face. One continuous shot, no cuts, no camera movement.

PART 1, THE CHEF (0.0 to 3.4 seconds): He wears the white double-breasted chef jacket from Image 1, buttoned, collar neat, sleeves rolled once, fitted to his shoulders. The background is the busy restaurant kitchen from Image 4, seen straight-on at his eye level: stainless steel counters and shelves, a ticket rail with order slips, steam rising from pots, warm tungsten light, two cooks in white moving quickly in soft focus behind him. The table in front of him is a stainless steel prep counter. The box in his hands is the stainless steel pan from Image 3, heaped with fresh, untouched pasta, bread and salad. As he tips the pan forward and down to the lower right, the food slides off its edge into a dented steel kitchen bin just below the bottom of the frame. He shakes his head with disappointment.

PART 2, THE TRANSFORMATION (3.4 to 4.4 seconds): Start the transformation exactly when his right hand touches his left shoulder, and finish it when his hand reaches his right hip. As his hand brushes down across his chest, the white chef jacket disintegrates into thousands of tiny glowing particles, bright white with an emerald green glow. They peel away along the path of his hand, swirl, and drift upward and out of frame like embers. Directly behind his hand, the navy overshirt from Image 2 materializes from the shoulders down, crisp and fitted, open over a plain dark t-shirt. At the same moment the kitchen behind him breaks into the same glowing particles and dissolves away, revealing the dining room from Image 5. The steel counter turns back into a wooden restaurant table. The effect is fast, clean and satisfying, complete in under one second. Nothing changes before his hand moves.

PART 3, THE OWNER (4.4 to 10.0 seconds): He wears the navy overshirt from Image 2 for the rest of the video, with no flicker back to the chef jacket. The background is the empty restaurant dining room from Image 5 after closing: wooden chairs flipped upside down on the tables, one warm pendant light glowing above him, dark windows, a calm, quiet after-hours mood, background in soft focus. He sits at a wooden table and speaks to the camera. He turns the open laptop toward the camera. The laptop screen shows Image 6 exactly: a dark screen with the green rounded-square leaf logo and the word FreshCast, sharp, bright and evenly lit. Keep the screen perfectly steady and readable for the final 1.5 seconds.

LOOK: Photorealistic, like it was filmed on a modern phone. Shallow depth of field, natural skin texture, realistic fabric folds, subtle film grain, practical lighting only: warm tungsten in the kitchen, a single warm pendant in the dining room. The glowing particles are the only visual effect.

AVOID: No text, captions, subtitles or watermarks. No extra logos. No extra people in front of him. No change to his face or body shape. No morphing hands. No camera shake added. No outfit change outside the 3.4 to 4.4 second window.
